Market research · July 2026

How Austria’s banks compete for Gen Z.

A market analysis of Erste Bank & Sparkasse, Raiffeisen, Bank Austria, BAWAG and the neobanks: how each one wins young customers, from the first touchpoint to the first investment.

0%of young Austrians keep their main account at a traditional universal bank
0%chose their first bank because their parents already banked there
€0bnwill be inherited in Austria over the next 30 years
0%of Gen Z would switch banks if another met their needs better

Sources: Bankenverband/BCG/Marketmind “Bankkunde Gen Z” (2025), AK / EU Joint Research Centre, ING/Visa Gen-Z study (2024).

INSIGHT 1

The free account is a must, not a strategy. Banks compete on everything around it, with bonuses tied to habits rather than to opening an account.

INSIGHT 2

The race starts years before the first account: in the classroom with financial education, and in the feed on TikTok.

INSIGHT 3

The end game is investing. Whoever holds Gen Z’s main account and first portfolio holds the inheritance generation.
