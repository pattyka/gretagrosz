QED

The maths that shows you the path.

QED is the maths app that shows you the path when you don’t know where to start, and gives you the structure to learn real problem solving one proof at a time.

Open QED

Let n = 2k + 1 be odd.✓

n² = 4k² + 4k + 1 = 4k(k+1) + 1✓

k(k+1) is even, so 8 | 4k(k+1)✓

Hence n² ≡ 1 (mod 8). ∎✓
