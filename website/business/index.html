What running it teaches me.

Small amounts, but the same questions a bank asks any business: what comes in, what goes out, and whether the customer comes back.

  • Pricing

    A price has to cover the material and my time, and still feel fair to the person paying it.

  • Customers

    At an event, people decide in seconds. Online, the photos and the listing do all the talking.

  • Costs

    Material is paid for before anything sells, and packaging and postage come off every order.

  • Promises

    An order is a promise. It ships on time, and the tattoos are ready before the event starts.
