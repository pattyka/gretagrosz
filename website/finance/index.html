How Austria’s banks compete for Gen Z.

A market analysis of Erste Bank & Sparkasse, Raiffeisen, Bank Austria, BAWAG and the neobanks: how each one wins young customers, from the first touchpoint to the first investment.

Sources: Bankenverband/BCG/Marketmind “Bankkunde Gen Z” (2025), AK / EU Joint Research Centre, ING/Visa Gen-Z study (2024).

  1. 1

    The free account is a must, not a strategy.

    Banks compete on everything around it, with bonuses tied to habits rather than to opening an account.

  2. 2

    The race starts years before the first account.

    In the classroom with financial education, and in the feed on TikTok.

  3. 3

    The end game is investing.

    Whoever holds Gen Z’s main account and first portfolio holds the inheritance generation.
